## Conclusions now warranted

1. **Power:** Simple electricity models can be useful, but the load and weather regime and any binding constraint determine how well they transfer and how risky they are. Low MAE and safe upper prediction are different criteria.
2. **Water physics:** Cooling-water response depends on the technology, heat routing, outdoor air, equipment controls and treatment. W2-Air and the tower are separate conditional scenarios.
3. **Sufficiency:** For the tested fixed direct-air architecture, C1 is an excellent surrogate for W2 and has zero conditional regret on the one Step 30 day. W2 is still needed when feasibility or controls change.
4. **Decisions:** Capacity feasibility, daily water volume, peak water feed and the grid-water cost trade-off are distinct. Improving one need not improve the others.
5. **External validation:** No synchronized facility-water observations verify W0, C1 or W2 onsite predictions. Groundwater externalities and multi-site planning have not been evaluated.
